change, restart, ISO mount, WIM/catalog/answer-media work, VM/VHDX start or mutation, credential
activity, checkpoint, candidate/fixture exposure, A12, Quality execution, WP4 provider use, merge,
publication, release, or cleanup.

the Product Owner accepts the completed packet and one conditional authorization envelope.
