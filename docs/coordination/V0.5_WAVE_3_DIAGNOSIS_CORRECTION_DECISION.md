## Decision

The Product Owner approves correction of the Wave 3 answer-media defect and rejects amendment
of the acceptance criteria. The correction is split into two gates so another installation is
not attempted before the existing failure is understood.

1. `V05-W3-02` is authorized for offline, diagnosis-only inspection of the preserved media,
   VHDXs, Windows Setup logs, XML, catalogs, and existing evidence.
2. `V05-W3-03` is conditionally authorized only after the Chief of Staff accepts a diagnosis
   that identifies a bounded correction and confirms the corrected proof can remain within the
   existing zero-NIC, synthetic-account, no-network architecture.

## Fixed acceptance boundary

The final proof still requires two byte-identical corrected answer ISOs and two fresh,
equivalent, answer-media-driven zero-NIC installations. Manual OOBE bypass, removal of answer
media, reuse of either prior synthetic installation, or acceptance by waiver is prohibited.

## Diagnosis controls

- Keep both preserved synthetic VMs powered off.
- Mount any VHDX read-only and record before/after identity.
- Do not edit XML, rebuild ISO media, create or boot a VM, or start an installation.
- Do not touch `Jarvis-A12-v05`, candidate artifacts, fixtures, credentials, or downstream gates.
- Return the exact Windows Setup failure, the responsible answer-file/media element, the
  proposed minimal correction, and a preflight matrix for the proof task.
- If evidence is missing, ambiguous, or points beyond a bounded answer-media correction, stop
  for CTO/Product Owner review.

## Proof controls

After diagnosis acceptance, the Chief of Staff may activate `V05-W3-03`. That task may build
corrected media twice and perform exactly two fresh synthetic installs in newly created Gen2
zero-NIC VMs. It has zero retries and must preserve all prior evidence.

This decision authorizes no network access, target-VM activity, real credentials, cleanup,
candidate or fixture exposure, A12, Quality, merge, publication, or release work.
