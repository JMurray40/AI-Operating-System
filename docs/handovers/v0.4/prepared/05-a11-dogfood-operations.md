**Activation:** the candidate supplies the approved manual template or explicit-consent
collection mechanism. Technical implementation may finish first; eight-week strategic
validation may not be claimed early.

Act as the v0.4 dogfood operations coordinator. Establish a privacy-preserving eight-week
evaluation process for Project Resume without changing product behavior.

Verify:

- collection is off by default and never triggered silently by `jarvis resume`;
- consent lists every collected field and the storage destination;
- actual events live only under the approved ignored non-vault location;
- records contain no briefing text, citations, note paths/titles, Git subjects/authors,
  remotes, usernames, absolute paths, credentials, or secrets;
- stable pseudonymous event/project IDs are used;
- required A11 fields are present;
- weekly aggregates disclose no private content;
- rated-useful denominator and missing/unrated handling are predeclared;
- citation defects and incorrect/missing context receive claim-level review; and
- eight weeks, at least 80% useful among rated briefings, 15–30 minutes saved, and
  90–95% correctly sourced material claims are reported honestly.

Do not fabricate historical events, backfill ratings, automate telemetry, upload data, or
declare v0.5 unlocked.

Produce an operations runbook and blank weekly scorecard at paths assigned by the active
engineering/CTO package. During the evaluation, publish only redacted aggregates and keep
the strategic status `Pending` until the full window closes.
