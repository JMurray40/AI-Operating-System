Research notes answer a specific question, cite current primary sources, compare alternatives, state limitations, and recommend whether an ADR is warranted. Research is evidence; an accepted ADR is the decision.

## Initial research queue

- Local secrets and credential storage on Windows.
- Safe Markdown/YAML parsing and atomic vault writes.
- Full-text and semantic indexing options.
- Provider gateway and role-routing options.
- Direct vault access versus an Obsidian companion plugin.
